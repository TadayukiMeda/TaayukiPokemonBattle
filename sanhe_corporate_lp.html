Quality Control

Four gates.
Zero tolerance.

Every unit passes through four independent quality checkpoints before it leaves our facility. This process is why our defect rate stays below 1%.

Factory Audit Accepted — by appointment
01

Raw Material Inspection

Every incoming hide, fabric, and hardware component is inspected against specification before entering production. Sub-standard materials are rejected at intake — they never reach the production floor.

02

In-Process Inspection

Stitching tension, edge finishing, and structural integrity are checked continuously during production. Defects are corrected at the source, not discovered at the end.

03

Final Inspection

Completed units are inspected 100% — not sampled. Every piece is measured against the approved spec sheet and original sample. Dimensional tolerance: ±1mm.

04

Pre-Shipment Inspection

Packaging, labeling, quantity count, and export documentation are verified before loading. The last checkpoint before the shipment leaves our hands.
